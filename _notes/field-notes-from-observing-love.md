A running list of small things: the behaviours I keep noticing around me that warm my heart or make me smile.

1. Two toothbrushes in the holder.
2. Waiting for them to wake up before you have breakfast or coffee.
3. Coffee for two. At some point you stop asking and simply make it for two.
4. Ordering for two.
5. Eating from the same plate instead of two.
6. At Blue Tokai, a family, or sometimes just a duet of parent and child, the child holding a small glass of hot chocolate.
7. The little ones outside Motherhood Hospital. Some only a few days old, carried to the car like something that might spill.
8. Couples on the noisy roads. One of them carrying the other's bag at the end of a long day.
9. Wearing each other's t-shirts and jackets. Something obviously not their size, worn like it's the most natural thing in the world.
10. Dragging them along to the things you want to do. They didn't want to come. They came anyway.
11. House parties, and the eye contact of love. Across the room, mid-conversation with someone else, a half-second check-in.
12. A couple curled up in the corner of a sofa, talking about something that could not possibly matter to anyone else in the room.
13. Chatting while folding clothes, yours and theirs, until it stops mattering whose.
14. Remembering things for them. The password, or where the charger is.
15. Sharing a blanket when there is a perfectly good second one right there.
16. A head resting on a shoulder.
17. Your room, smelling of them.
18. Cooking for the people you love.
19. Carrying their phone and wallet without being asked, because traditionals rarely come with pockets.
20. Little ones holding on to the end of your t-shirt or jacket or chunni with their tiny hands.
