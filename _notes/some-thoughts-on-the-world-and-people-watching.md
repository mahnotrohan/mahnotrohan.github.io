title: Some thoughts on the world, and people watching
date: 2026-10-21
---

1. Most people are terrible listeners, and you're one more often than you'd think. That's okay. Notice how much you talk versus how much others talk to you, and aim for them to do twice the talking.
2. Think a lot, and write a lot. Process your thoughts and your emotions, then get them out of your head and your body: every day if you can, once a week at minimum. This might just be the answer to your absurd circadian rhythm, or your poop schedule (or the lack of one).
3. Be kind to kids. Don't raise your voice; teach them to handle conflict with tenderness. Aggression only ever works in cinema.
4. You'll lose people to many things: age, illness, errands, partners, marriage, trauma. Miss them, and move forward anyway.
5. Love more. Love like you don't know what's enough. But first, love yourself: work out what that means for you, and practice it.
6. Understand boundaries, yours and other people's. They are not walls. They are for you, and they hold regardless of the audience or the situation.
7. Know what you know, and stay open to what you don't, even though you don't know what you don't know.
8. Stay healthy. Physically - strength, mobility, endurance. Mentally - emotional regulation, sleep, a safe space to talk.
9. People won't tell you a lot of things. Learn to be okay with that. It's almost always about them, not you.
10. Attention allocation is an art. Allocate more attention to art than you think is enough.
11. Your job pays in many currencies: money, equity, network, health, skills. It can also run you into debt, health most commonly, relationships next. Trade-offs are fine, but don't let them become debts: those are hard to pay off, and you don't want them written off.
12. You'll never make the perfect cortado. Keep trying anyway. Life is the pursuit of a cortado: not the best one, but many wonderful ones.
13. Make the chilli, dude. Go and make the chilli.
14. People are their own punishment. Getting back at them is rarely worth it.
15. You deserve respect, no matter how big or how silly the fuck-up. You never deserve bullying, least of all at work.
16. What you learn never goes to waste. Neither does the effort it took.
