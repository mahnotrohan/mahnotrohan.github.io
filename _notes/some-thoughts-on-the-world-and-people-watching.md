title: Some thoughts on the world, and people watching
date: 2026-10-28
---

1. Process your thoughts and your emotions, then get them out of your head and your body. Every day if you can. This might just be the answer to your absurd sleep cycle, or your poop schedule (or the lack of one).
2. Most people are terrible listeners, and you're one more often than you'd think. That's okay. Notice how much you talk versus how much others talk to you, and aim for them to do more talking.
3. Be kind to kids. Teach them to handle conflict with tenderness. Aggression only ever works in cinema.
4. You'll lose people to many things - age, illness, errands, partners, marriage, trauma. You can miss them, and continue with your life.
5. Love more. Love like you don't know what's enough. But first, love yourself: work out what that means for you, and practice it.
6. Understand boundaries, yours and others'. They are not walls. They are for you, and they hold regardless of the audience.
7. Know what you know, and stay open to what you don't, even though you don't know what you don't know.
8. Try to stay healthy. Both Physically, and Mentally. If you do it the right way, it will take up a lot of time. But its an investment.
9. People won't tell you a lot of things. Learn to be okay with that. It's almost always about them, not you.
10. Attention allocation is an art. Allocate more attention to art than you think you should.
11. Your job pays in many currencies: money, equity, network, health, skills. It can also run you into debt, health most commonly, relationships next. Trade - offs are fine, but don't let them become debts. Relational debts hard to pay off (self or otherwise), and you probably don't want them written off.
12. You'll never make the perfect cortado. Keep trying anyway. Life is the pursuit of a cortado: not the best one, but many wonderful ones.
13. Make the chilli, dude. Go and make the chilli.
14. People are their own punishment. Getting back at them is rarely worth it.
15. You deserve respect, no matter how big or how silly the fuck-up. You never deserve bullying, least of all at work.
16. What you learn never goes to waste. Neither does the effort it took.
