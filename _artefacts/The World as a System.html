A worldview, written on the way out of the dark

The world is a huge, spectacularly huge system.

Like any system, it has its stocks and flows. This is what it looks like to someone who spent a long time in the shadows, can finally think, is scared, and has decided to step out anyway.

Scroll. The light comes up as you go. Everything here was true in the dark, too. It was just harder to see.

Boundary

You have to draw a boundary.

The universe is unimaginatively big. A system only becomes something you can think about once you decide what is inside it and what is out. Nothing in the world hands you that line. You draw it.

The circle forming behind these words is not the world. It is the part you chose to look at.

Influence

Everything in this world influences everything.

Which is why the notion of future speculation, and of control, is an illusion. The threads between things are too many to trace, and they keep moving while you are counting them.

Projection

But what you project into the world influences it.

A lot more than you think. In more places than you notice. And long before you get to see it.

Watch the rings leaving the small warm point at the centre. They travel further than the light does.

Two axioms

Two principles drive almost everything.

They are close to axiomatic. It is very important to understand both, and the second more deeply than the first.

I · Theory of constraints

Any system moves only as fast as its narrowest point. Find the constraint and you have found where the system actually lives. A great deal of the political and economic shape of the world is this principle wearing different clothes.

Forms the world outside
II · Vigilance against self-preservation

The mind is built to keep you alive, and it will trade almost anything to do it, including your growth, your honesty, and your ability to see clearly. The guard is useful. The guard also has to be watched.

Models the brain within · understand this one more deeply

Self

The study of the human mind is the study of a wedge of something bigger.

That bigger thing is the concept of self. To understand it, and the society you live in, there are seven things to understand. Not as a checklist. As a system: each one feeds the others.

Meaning making, and luck

The meaning of life is what you give it. And once you assign it one, you figure out the rest.

A lot of life is random, and your fortune is out of your hands. But you can widen the surface that luck can land on. Expose yourself to more things. Do more things. Travel. Put yourself out there, in the world. The more you expose yourself, the higher your chances. Be safe, look after yourself, and go.
